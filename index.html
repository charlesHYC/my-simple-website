About

I am an electrical engineering student working on digital hardware design, from cell-based flows through to FPGA systems.

My current work is an FPGA engine that stores recorded network traffic in High Bandwidth Memory and replays it onto a 100 Gbps link, either preserving the original capture timing or running as fast as the wire allows.
